Help

How to borrow a book

  1. Click Sign In, then Create account, and fill in the form.
  2. Wait until a librarian approves your library card.
  3. Search for the book. If it is on loan, sign in and reserve it.
  4. Bring your card to the desk. The librarian scans your card and the book.

Common questions

How do I renew a book?

Sign in, open My Books and click Renew.

How do I pay a fine?

Pay at the library desk.

I forgot my password.

Click Forgot password? on the Sign In page.
